Sign conventions in cp_lib/geometry.py, measured on the 2025 season (not assumed).
Written by Task 2 Step 5 of docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-13-contact-point-plan.md.

1. attack_direction is signed toward PULL. SETTLED.

   Correlation of attack_direction with spray angle measured toward right field
   (positive), on 2025 balls in play with hit coordinates:

       stand R   +0.316   n = 67,351
       stand L   -0.290   n = 52,101

   The two signs are opposite, which is what a pull-signed angle does: pull is
   left field for a right-handed batter and right field for a left-handed one.
   Had attack_direction been signed in field x, both correlations would have been
   positive. So bat_frame keeps the pull_sign multiplication on the x component
   of the swing velocity, and attack_direction_pull (the field-x-signed version)
   is carried as a separate feature.

2. intercept_ball_minus_batter_pos_x_inches is ALREADY body-relative and is
   never negative. SETTLED, and it corrects the plan.

       stand R   min 6.3   median 37.2   max 75.8   corr with plate_x  +0.929
       stand L   min 6.2   median 36.7   max 79.3   corr with plate_x  -0.903

   The column is the lateral distance from the batter to the ball, positive away
   from the body, with the handedness already taken out: the plate_x correlation
   flips sign by stand precisely because the column does not. The plan defined
   icpt_x_body as icpt_x * -pull_sign, which would have made every left-handed
   batter's value negative for no reason. icpt_x_body is therefore icpt_x itself,
   and the field-x displacement from the batter to the ball is carried separately
   as icpt_x_field = icpt_x * -pull_sign. along_bat_from_com already used the
   field-x form and is unchanged.

   intercept_ball_minus_batter_pos_y_inches runs -12.0 to 72.0, median 29.2.

3. swing_path_tilt lean (which way the swing plane tilts): NOT SETTLED BY
   GEOMETRY. Left to the Task 4 grid search against Savant's own sav_z.

   tilt_sign = +1 puts the barrel below the hands, tilt_sign = -1 above. Three
   measurements disagree with each other:

   a. Displacement of the ball out of the plane between the intercept depth and
      the plate (the cheap z proxy), 2025 swings:

          tilt_sign -1   in play  median |z| 0.79 in   whiff median 1.13 in
          tilt_sign +1   in play  median |z| 1.35 in   whiff median 4.40 in

      Savant publishes in-play swings as 99.5 percent lined up (|z| <= 2) and
      whiffs as 13 percent lined up, so the contrast it reports is large. Only
      tilt_sign +1 produces a large contrast; tilt_sign -1 makes whiffs and balls
      in play look almost the same, which Savant says they are not.

   b. Angle between the batted ball's initial direction and the swing plane, on
      balls in play:

          tilt_sign -1   median 17.2 deg out of plane, 26.6 deg along the bat axis
          tilt_sign +1   median 24.1 deg out of plane, 18.8 deg along the bat axis

      This favours tilt_sign -1 on "the ball leaves in the swing plane" and
      tilt_sign +1 on "the ball leaves perpendicular to the bat axis", which is
      the more physical of the two statements for an oblique bat-ball collision.
      On hard-hit balls (EV at or above 100 mph, n = 29,381) the two are within
      one degree of each other (14.6 against 15.5), so this measurement does not
      separate them.

   c. Physical depiction: MLB draws swing path tilt with the bat head below the
      hands, which is tilt_sign +1.

   Conclusion: geometry.TILT_SIGN defaults to +1 on (a) and (c), and
   candidate_features takes tilt_sign as an argument so Task 4 can grid-search it
   against Savant's published per-swing sav_z, which is the only arbiter that
   settles it. See data/reports/discovery_2025.md for the outcome.
